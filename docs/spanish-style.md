# Spanish Writing Style

Spanish in this project (manifesto, website, tutorials, outreach, **and institution-facing technical documents** per [language-policy.md](language-policy.md)) must read as **native Spanish, never as translated English**. Every rule below comes from a real correction in this project.

## Banned patterns (calques and false friends)

| Wrong (English-in-Spanish) | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "fuente de verdad" | "la versión oficial / de referencia" | calque of "source of truth" |
| "vive en el repositorio" | "está en el repositorio" | calque of "lives in" |
| "país análogo" | "país analógico" | *análogo* = analogous (false friend) |
| "ese es el punto" | "de eso se trata" | calque of "that's the point" |
| "La evidencia no es teórica" | "Esto no es teoría" | *evidence* → "pruebas"; anglicized register |
| "sus fallas viven años" | "sus fallas pasan años escondidas" | calque of "live for years" |
| "tu plata llegando a cualquier cuenta" | "que tu plata llegue a cualquier cuenta" | English gerund pattern after noun |
| "comercian sus fallas" | "comercian con sus fallas" | *comerciar* requires *con* |
| "el roadmap" | "la hoja de ruta" | unnecessary anglicism in public copy |
| "van a ganar a los clientes" | "se van a llevar a los clientes" | unidiomatic |
| "en cualquier lugar, a cualquier hora, y sin costo" | "…a cualquier hora y sin costo" | no Oxford comma in Spanish |
| trailing "…, hoy" | "así es como vivimos hoy…" | English "…, today" pattern |

## Register

- Ecuadorian, warm, direct: "la plata", "toca esperar", "la señora del mercado", "el ciudadano de a pie" are good. Corporate/consultant Spanish is not.
- Register shifts with audience. In outreach, prefer plain Spanish: "conector" over "gateway", "muchísimo más barato" over "órdenes de magnitud más barato". In **technical documents written in Spanish**, the standard's own vocabulary always wins — write *llave*, *malla*, *por conciliar*, *empadronamiento*, *irrevocabilidad*, and keep protocol identifiers untranslated (`pacs.008`, `ACSP`, `timeout` when quoting the catálogo, which uses it). Don't invent Spanish for terms the BCE left in English, and don't leave English where the BCE has a Spanish term.
- Idioms welcome when natural: "buscarle la quinta pata al gato", "picos y palas".

## Verification

Read it aloud. If a sentence sounds like it was thought in English, rewrite it. When in doubt, ask: "how would someone say this over coffee in Quito?"
