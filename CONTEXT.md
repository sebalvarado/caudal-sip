# Ledger and payments

The language for how money moves inside Caudal. Scheme terms from the regulation (llave, integrante, red de pagos, the catálogo states) stay in [`docs/glossary.md`](docs/glossary.md). This file only records distinctions we had to draw ourselves.

## Language

**Ledger**:
The book of record for scheme obligations and for each integrante's headroom. A change to it is atomic across both. Where a payment and the ledger disagree, the ledger is right.
_Avoid_: balance table, wallet, the payment

**Payment**:
The process of moving a sum from one integrante to another. It asks the ledger to reserve, post, or void, and it remembers the reservation, the payment id, and the messages. Where it stands is read from the ledger and those messages. It is not the book of record, and it keeps no status of its own.
_Avoid_: transaction, the aggregate, a status column

**Reservation**:
A hold the ledger has placed, tying an integrante's headroom to a pending obligation, until the ledger posts it or voids it. Time passing does not end it. The payment id identifies it, and reserving the same payment id again returns the same hold.
_Avoid_: lock, authorization, a payment status, a new id per attempt

**Projection**:
A reading of where payments stand, built from the ledger and the messages. It can be thrown away and built again. It is not the book of record.
_Avoid_: a status column, a second ledger

**Payment id**:
The catálogo's unique code for one payment, generated by the ordering integrante and beginning with its BIC, so it is unique across the network. A repeat is the same payment only when the amount, the beneficiary's BIC, and the resolved account are unchanged. Any of those differing is refused, and the first hold stands. The fee is not compared: the scheme quoted it.
_Avoid_: end-to-end id (a different ISO field), a ledger-minted id, a fresh id on timeout

**Refusal**:
The ledger's final no: nothing was committed, and the payment may act on it.
_Avoid_: error, failure, timeout

**Unknown outcome**:
A call that ended without saying whether it committed. It is answered by asking again, never by assuming a refusal.
_Avoid_: timeout as rejection, failed, rolled back

**Replay**:
A repeated ledger command that finds the books already holding it. The books do not change. It does not mean the caller has already sent its message.
_Avoid_: a second posting, a cached response, already sent

**Reversal**:
A new ledger entry that mirrors every leg of a transfer and leaves the original in place. A posted payment is not reversed: it is irrevocable, and only a void ends a reservation that has not been posted.
_Avoid_: void, delete, an update, a hand-built mirror that drops a leg

**Unlisted reason**:
A reason code a counterparty sent that the catálogo does not list. It is kept as evidence and never sent by us.
_Avoid_: unknown code, other, a code of our own

**Scheme time**:
The civil time the catálogo's hours are written in: operating hours, cut-offs, the 18:00 close, the risk week, and the date inside a payment id.
_Avoid_: server time, local time

**Business date**:
The business day whose malla a payment belongs to. A payment ordered outside operating hours belongs to the next business day.
_Avoid_: value date, the calendar date of the payment
